# PulseIQ

## Tagline
Decisions, Backed by Data.

---

## Overview

PulseIQ is an AI-powered Business Intelligence Platform designed for small and medium businesses. It transforms raw business data into clear dashboards, actionable insights, and intelligent recommendations.

---

## Vision

To make business analytics simple, accessible, and useful for every small business owner.

---

## Target Users

- Retail Stores
- Cafes & Restaurants
- Bakeries
- Medical Stores
- Salons
- Gyms
- Clothing Stores
- Electronics Stores

---

## Problem Statement

Small businesses generate a lot of data but often struggle to understand it. Existing BI tools are expensive or too complex.

PulseIQ simplifies business analytics with an easy-to-use dashboard and AI-powered insights.

---

## Solution

PulseIQ enables businesses to:

- Upload business data
- View analytics dashboards
- Monitor business health
- Identify trends
- Generate reports
- Ask PRISM business-related questions

---

## Core Values

- Simplicity
- Speed
- Accuracy
- Professional Experience
- Business First
- AI as an Assistant, not the Main Product

---

## Project Goal

Build a Business Intelligence Platform that looks and feels like a real SaaS product rather than a student project.
